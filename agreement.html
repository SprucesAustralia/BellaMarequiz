🗝 1. Keys, fobs and building access

✓ Read

New rule — effective 19 September 2026

Any key, fob or swipe card issued to you must be returned within 3 business days of your last shift, of going away, or of us asking for it back. If it is not returned in that time, $30 per key is deducted from your next pay.

  • •Keys and fobs stay the property of Bella Mare. You are holding them, not keeping them.
  • •Never hand your key to anyone else — not a friend, not another cleaner, not a partner. Whoever has it, it is still your name on it and still your deduction.
  • •If a key is lost or damaged, tell us the same day. Bella Mare charges us for cut keys, and that cost is passed on.
  • •The $30 is a flat deduction per key per pay run and keeps applying each pay run until the key is back.
  • •Hand keys back to Leo or to the Bella Mare office and get it confirmed in writing (a WhatsApp message is fine). If you do not have confirmation, we have no record of it.
  • •Business days are Monday to Friday, Queensland time, not counting public holidays.

Why this exists: an unreturned key has held up a pay run and cost us a re-cut. This rule makes the outcome predictable for everybody instead of a dispute each time.

📶 2. Finishing a room in the app

✓ Read
  • •Hit Finish in the app the moment you leave the room — on the day, not the next morning. A room that is never marked finished does not appear on your pay.
  • •Photos are required at Finish. The hotel checks the room against your photos. No photo means the hotel cannot sign it off, which can cost you the $10 bonus.
  • •If a photo will not upload — it happens, it is an app fault — post it in the team group the same day so it is on record.
  • •Only mark a room finished if you actually cleaned it.
  • •If you clean a room that is not on your list, add it in the app and say so in the group. It gets paid once Leo approves it.

👤 3. Who gets paid for a room

✓ Read
  • •The Finish screen decides the pay, not WhatsApp. Agreeing to a room in a chat is a plan; finishing it in the app is the fact.
  • •If you work as a pair, a room shows on both names. At Finish, say who actually did it. The money follows that answer.
  • •If you join the app part-way through a week, your earlier rooms may be sitting under your partner’s name. Tell us and we will move them.
  • •We will never move or reduce your pay on someone else’s word. We will always ask you first.
  • •Training: the trainer takes two thirds of the room and the trainee one third, out of the same room rate.

📅 4. Availability — keep it true

✓ Read
  • •Your availability in the app is what we roster from. Update it yourself, in advance. We do not assign anyone who has marked a day unavailable.
  • •A day left showing “available” that you cannot actually work is a no-show, even if you never said yes to a specific room.
  • •If you take a job somewhere else for a day, mark that day off here first.
  • •If you need to drop a day you had accepted, say so as early as you can, in the app and in the group. Early is fine. Silent is not.
  • •Same-day cancellations and no-shows are the ones that cost money — see section 11.

⏰ 5. Timing and back-to-back rooms

✓ Read
  • •We run on Queensland time. Every date and time in the app and in this agreement is QLD, whatever your phone says.
  • •A starred room is back-to-back: a guest checks in that same afternoon. It must be finished before 2pm.
  • •Do not start later than 10am on a day with starred rooms. If you cannot be there by 10am, tell us the night before.
  • •Rough guide: 2-bed apartment 1.5 hrs · 2-bed villa 1.75–2 hrs · 3-bed 2 hrs · service clean 30 min.
  • •Allow 5–7 minutes for arrival and parking. Park in the basement, entrance on Boundary St.

✨ 6. Quality, checks and fix-ups

✓ Read

Our promise to the hotel is that they do not need to check behind us. Nobody supervises you — the hotel’s rating of each room does, and it decides the money. Every departure already carries a $10 “perfect” bonus inside your rate.

Hotel’s ratingWhat it meansYouHotel
5★ PerfectGood clean$10 bonus stays—
4★ Fix-upOne thing to go back for−$10 (the bonus)$3 off
1–3★ RedoNot to standard−$15$10 off
  • •You go back and fix it, the same day. The fix-up appears on your room card in the app with exactly what was missed; fix it and tap Fixed ✓. The hotel sees that you did.
  • •If you do not go back — the hotel fixes it, or nobody has by the end of the day — the amounts double: −$20 for a fix-up, −$30 for a redo (and the hotel’s credit doubles too, for their time). The doubling is automatic at the end of the day.
  • •One freebie, once in your time with us: your first-ever fix-up or redo costs you nothing, provided you go back and fix it. It is meant for someone new. Anyone who already had fix-ups before 22 September 2026 has used theirs.
  • •Streak bonus: every 25 perfect departures in a row (counted from 22 September 2026) pays an extra $25 on the 25th room. A fix-up or redo restarts the count. Your progress is on My pay › My quality.
  • •Not checked within 24 hours = perfect. If the hotel has not checked a room within 24 hours of your Finish, the app approves it 5★ and you keep the bonus.
  • •Wrong rating? Ratings can be changed by the hotel until the pay run. Tap Ask the hotel to look again on the room in the app and say why — do not argue it on WhatsApp. When a rating changes, the money follows it.
  • •The checklist is the standard, not a suggestion. The things that come back most often: ovens, tinfoil on the drip trays, dishwashers left loaded, balcony glass. Rushing is what shows up in the photos.
  • •If something in a room is broken, stained or missing when you arrive, photograph it before you start (Report condition in the app). That protects you.
  • •Where two people clean a room together, the room’s deduction is shared in the same proportion as the pay, and the freebie applies only if both still have theirs.

💰 7. How and when you get paid

✓ Read
  • •The pay week runs Monday to Sunday. It is paid the following Friday.
  • •You are paid per room, by room type and day — the full rate card is on the Payments page.
  • •Only rooms that are finished in the app and approved by the hotel are paid.
  • •You are an independent contractor and invoice under your own ABN. No tax is withheld, no TFN is held. Your tax and super are yours.
  • •Deductions that can appear on a pay run: unreturned keys ($30 each, section 1), a re-cut key at cost, and cost recovery under section 11. Anything deducted is itemised and explained before it is paid.

🏦 8. Your bank details

✓ Read
  • •Change your own bank details in the app. Do not send them to anyone in a message.
  • •Every change is checked with you before the next pay run. We will ask you to repeat the BSB and the last four digits. Expect that question — it is how we know the change was really you.
  • •Your pay for that week is held until you confirm. Answer the same day and nothing is delayed.
  • •We will never ask you for a full account number, a password or a code over WhatsApp. If a message asks for that, it is not us. Screenshot it and send it to Leo.
  • •No bank details in the app means you cannot be included in a payment run.

🛍 9. Guests, their property and privacy

✓ Read
  • •Anything left behind goes to the Bella Mare office the same day — cash, chargers, jewellery, clothes, food, all of it. Nothing goes home with you, however small, however likely it is to be thrown out.
  • •Photograph anything valuable where you found it before you move it.
  • •Photos are for room condition only. No photos of guests, of guests’ belongings, or of documents.
  • •Do not post anything about a guest, a room or the building on social media.
  • •If a guest is still in a room you were told was vacant, leave, close the door and message the group.

🚪 10. Going away, or leaving the team

✓ Read
  • •Travelling, moving on, or taking a break — all fine. Tell us as early as you can, and at the latest before your last rostered day, so the roster can be covered.
  • •Mark your days off in the app as well as telling us, so nothing is assigned to you.
  • •Return your keys within 3 business days of your last shift (section 1). This is the one that most often holds up a final pay.
  • •Make sure every room you did is finished in the app before you go. We cannot pay for a room that was never marked done.
  • •Your final pay goes out on the normal Friday cycle once keys are back and your rooms are reconciled.
  • •If you come back later, your profile is still here.

⚠ 11. Cost recovery for operational disruption

✓ Read

Where your actions or omissions cost the company money, those costs may be passed on to you. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • •Poor or incomplete work needing a re-clean, another inspection, or compensation to a guest.
  • •Abandoning a shift or leaving a job unfinished without reasonable notice or approval.
  • •Repeated roster changes inside 48 hours of a shift.
  • •Same-day cancellations and no-shows, which cost the most because cover has to be found immediately.

Recoverable costs may include:

  • •Administration and coordination — re-doing the roster, ringing round, managing the fallout.
  • •Recruitment — sourcing and onboarding a replacement at short notice.
  • •Emergency incentives paid to someone else to fill the gap.
  • •Any other direct cost reasonably incurred because of it.

We understand that emergencies happen. This section is about avoidable disruption, not about a genuine emergency. If something goes wrong, tell us as early as you can and we will work it out with you.

💬 12. Your status, and how we talk

✓ Read

  • •There is no guaranteed number of rooms. Work depends on what the hotel has.
